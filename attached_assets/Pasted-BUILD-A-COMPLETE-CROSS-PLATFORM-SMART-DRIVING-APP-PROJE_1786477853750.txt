BUILD A COMPLETE CROSS-PLATFORM SMART DRIVING APP

PROJECT TYPE:
Mobile application for iOS and Android.

PRODUCT CONCEPT:
Build a modern "Smart Driving Companion" inspired by the general category of GPS speedometer / HUD / trip tracker applications.

IMPORTANT:
The uploaded reference screenshot is only a functional/design reference for understanding the type of application we want to build. DO NOT copy its branding, name, logo, proprietary graphics, screenshots, exact UI, or copyrighted visual assets.

We want our own original product with a modern 2026-quality interface and significantly more useful functionality.

The application should combine:

1. GPS Speedometer
2. Speed-limit awareness
3. Full-screen driving mode
4. HUD mode
5. Live map
6. Trip tracking
7. Trip history
8. Fuel/cost tracking
9. Vehicle management
10. Driving analytics
11. Safety features
12. Parking location
13. AI-powered trip insights
14. Maintenance reminders
15. Future wearable / CarPlay / Android Auto support

The application must be designed mobile-first and optimized for safe, glanceable use while driving.

==================================================
1. PRODUCT PRINCIPLES
==================================================

The app must feel like a premium driving utility, not an old-fashioned speedometer.

Design goals:

- Extremely simple
- Fast
- Modern
- Premium

- Dark driving interface
